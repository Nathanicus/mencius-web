Mencius Support

Thanks for using Mencius, a focused way to learn the 3,000 most common Chinese characters and the full HSK vocabulary.

Need help?
Email nate@mencius.app and we’ll get back to you. Including your iOS version and a screenshot helps us help you faster.

Does my progress sync across devices?

Yes. As long as you’re signed into the same iCloud account, your progress, favourites, and notes sync automatically through your private iCloud. On a new device, sign in with the same Apple ID and your data restores.

How do I restore my purchase?

Go to Settings → Restore purchase (also available on the upgrade screen). Make sure you’re signed into the Apple ID you bought with.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Open Settings → Manage subscription, or go to the iOS Settings app → your name → Subscriptions. Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled; cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid the next charge.

What’s included free vs. Premium?

The free tier includes the full study experience for your first 100 cards, characters and words combined, plus daily writing practice, both games, and every catalog free to browse. Everything you learn stays reviewable forever. Premium unlocks all 3,000 characters and the full HSK vocabulary, exact-level HSK decks, custom words, pro review controls, streak repairs, advanced statistics, and every theme.

How do I delete my account and data?

Go to Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your profile, progress, favourites, and notes from your device and your iCloud. It cannot be undone.
